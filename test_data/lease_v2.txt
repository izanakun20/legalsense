RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT — SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT
This fictional document is created only for testing LegalSense.

1. PARTIES
Landlord: Alex Morgan
Tenant: Jordan Lee
Property: 14 Cedar Lane, Sample City

2. TERM
The lease begins on January 1, 2027 and ends on December 31, 2027.
The tenant must provide written notice at least 60 days before moving out.

3. RENT
Monthly rent is INR 27,000, payable on or before the 5th day of each month.
A late fee of INR 1,000 may be charged if rent is received after the 5th day.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The tenant shall pay a security deposit of INR 54,000.
The deposit may be used for unpaid rent or documented property damage.
The remaining amount should be returned within 45 days after move-out.

5. MAINTENANCE
The landlord is responsible for structural repairs and major plumbing or electrical defects.
The tenant is responsible for routine cleaning and damage caused by the tenant's negligence.

6. EARLY TERMINATION
Either party may request early termination by giving 60 days' written notice.
If the tenant leaves without the required notice, the landlord may retain an amount equal to two months' rent from the security deposit, subject to the terms of this agreement.

7. RENEWAL
The lease automatically renews for one additional year unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 45 days before the end date.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
The parties will first attempt to resolve disputes through direct discussion.
If the dispute remains unresolved, the parties may seek professional legal assistance.

9. GENERAL
This synthetic agreement is for software testing only and does not represent legal advice.
