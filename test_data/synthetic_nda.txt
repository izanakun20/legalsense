MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT — SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT
For LegalSense testing only.

1. PURPOSE
The parties may exchange confidential business and technical information for evaluating a hypothetical software partnership.

2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
Confidential Information includes non-public product plans, source code, pricing information, customer lists, security documentation, and business strategies disclosed in writing or marked confidential.

3. OBLIGATIONS
The receiving party shall use Confidential Information only for the stated purpose.
The receiving party shall restrict access to personnel who need the information for that purpose.
The receiving party shall use reasonable safeguards to protect Confidential Information.

4. EXCLUSIONS
Information is not Confidential Information if it was publicly available before disclosure, becomes public without breach of this agreement, or was lawfully known by the receiving party before disclosure.

5. DURATION
The confidentiality obligations begin on the date of signing and continue for three years after the last disclosure of Confidential Information.

6. RETURN OR DELETION
Upon written request, the receiving party shall return or delete Confidential Information, except for copies retained automatically by routine backup systems.

7. NO LICENSE
No intellectual-property license is granted by disclosure of Confidential Information.

8. GENERAL
This synthetic document is for software testing only. It is not legal advice.
